Usage

Keeping Pho happy

Pho works on every brand color. Always use the transparent PNG cutouts, never a screenshot with a box around it.

Do

  • Keep her recognizable: cream bowl, yellow ears, navy outfit, yellow sash
  • Show her cooking, serving, welcoming or enjoying food
  • Leave clear space around her, at least the width of one ear

Don’t

  • Stretch, squash or recolor her
  • Add extra limbs or floating ingredients
  • Use realistic food textures or aggressive expressions
